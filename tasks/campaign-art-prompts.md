# Campaign image generation

Built-in image_gen tool. Reference: Assets/Images/ChatGPT Image 7 июл. 2026 г., 16_38_50.png.

Four 5 × 4 atlases, with 78 of 80 scenes used by levels 23–100. Two extra scenes remain available in the fourth atlas. Backgrounds are part of the gameplay painting. The earlier isolated-icon proposals were rejected and are not included.

## Atlas 1

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE illustrated atlas of 20 full-bleed square level paintings for an existing sand puzzle game.
Input image: reference only, showing the game's established art direction. Create NEW subjects with the same sophisticated illustrated adventure-card style. Do not repeat its characters.
Layout: EXACTLY 5 columns by 4 rows, all 20 cells equal size. 2000x1600 or larger landscape canvas with 5:4 aspect. No margins, no gutters, no lines between tiles. Every cell is its own complete SQUARE scene, no object crossing tiles.
Style: match reference's rich clean hand-drawn cel illustration, strong dark contours, bold color masses, 8-12 coherent colors per scene, interesting detailed backgrounds with foreground/middle/background depth. This is NOT pixel art and NOT an icon sheet. Mature all-ages adventure illustrations, expressive faces, handsome elegant compositions, no baby/chibi proportions. Each tile fills edge to edge with colorful environment, NEVER blank, white, solid color, or transparent backgrounds.
Subjects row-major:
1 snow leopard against snowy mountain peaks and pine silhouettes; 2 red deer antlers in golden autumn woodland; 3 raccoon by a moonlit creek; 4 panda with tall bamboo and jade hills; 5 lynx in sunlit birch forest.
6 dolphin leaping before waves and distant islands; 7 sea turtle among coral and shafts of underwater light; 8 octopus by a sunken ship and seaweed; 9 seahorse beside coral towers; 10 polar bear at an aurora-lit icy fjord.
11 peacock with fanned tail in palace garden; 12 kingfisher on branch over a blue river; 13 toucan in tropical canopy; 14 flamingo in sunset lagoon; 15 phoenix against a volcanic sunset.
16 coiled emerald dragon beside mossy castle ruins; 17 white unicorn in moonlit flower meadow; 18 griffin on windy mountain ledge; 19 fire salamander on glowing rocks; 20 stag spirit with glowing antlers in misty woods.
Constraints: recognizable large main subject, attractive painterly background occupying entire tile, no words, text, digits, watermarks or empty margins. Keep composition readable when reduced to 64x64 game cells, avoid excessive microtexture.
```
